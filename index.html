Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal. This classic omlette combines beaten eggs cooked to perfection, optionally filled with your choice of cheese, vegetables, or meat.

    Preparation Time

  • Total: Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes

    Ingredients

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • Salt to taste
  • Pepper to taste
  • 1-2 tablespoons of butter or oil
  • Optional fillings: cheese, diced vegetables, cooked meats, herbs

    Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs in a bowl with the pinch of salt and pepper until they are well mixed. You can also add a tablespoon of milk or cream for a fluffier texture.
  2. Heat the pan over medium heat and add the butter or oil.
  3. Once the butter is melted and bubbling, pour in the eggs. Tilt the pan to ensure the eggs are evenly distributed.
  4. When the eggs are set around the edges, gently push them toward the center with a spatula.
  5. Once cooked to your liking, slide the omelette onto a plate and serve immediately.
